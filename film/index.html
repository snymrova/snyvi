snyvi

A desktop app with one desk for each project, and everything your agents write, kept where you can read it.

A desk for each project

Its folder, and up to four agents side by side, each in its own panel.

Nothing scrolls away

Plans and reviews land on the desk, marked with the panel that wrote them, and open as clean pages.

Filed, and kept

Every document filed by project in one inbox, every revision kept. C shows what changed.

Out of your head

Each desk keeps its own notes, so what is left to do stays with the project.

Just as you left it

Every desk keeps its place. ⌘K finds anything, across all of them.

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, and anything else that speaks MCP.
