Panels in the back

24 Oct 2026 · snyvi 1.49

A desk in snyvi holds four panels. Four turned out to be the right number for agents: enough to keep a few things moving, few enough that you can still see what each one is doing.

Then the dev server moved in. It needs a terminal, it needs to stay running, and it took a slot. So did the test watcher on another desk. Four agents became three, and the grid filled up with things I didn't need to look at.

What's new

Any panel can now go to the back. Four stay in the grid and up to four more wait behind it. A panel in the back keeps running, and the desk's head shows a small chip for it. Click the chip and the panel slides in as a drawer over the grid. Close the drawer and nothing on the desk has moved.

You can move a panel back from its menu or with ⌃⌥⇧B, and ⌃⌥B opens the drawer. Or start one there with + In the back… and the command it should run. An agent can suggest one too: when it wants a dev server, it proposes a panel in the back and you approve it.

When something dies back there

A server that crashed in a panel you can't see is the worst kind of failure, because you find out ten minutes later from a blank page in your browser. So a panel in the back that exits with an error turns its chip amber and counts as needs you, the same as an agent waiting on a question. The desk's dot in the sidebar says so too.

What I left out

I thought about tabs: a tab view, a tab per thread, documents in tabs. What I actually needed was room for things that run without being watched, not another way to switch. Tabs might still come, but this didn't need them.

Try it

snyvi is free, runs on your machine, and has no account. It's here for Linux, macOS and Windows, and the source is on GitHub.
