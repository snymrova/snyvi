Copy keeps the Markdown

27 Oct 2026 · snyvi 1.53

Most of what I read in snyvi was written by an agent: a plan, a review, a list of what's left. Most of what I do with it is take part of it somewhere else. Step 3 goes back to Claude with "do this one". A table goes into an issue. The commands go into a terminal.

That used to go badly. Select across a few paragraphs, copy, paste into Claude, and what arrived was the page as text. The bullets were gone, the headings were plain lines, a code block lost its fence, and a table came out as one cell after another. I'd fix it by hand, or open the source and copy from there.

Two kinds on the clipboard

Now a copy from a Markdown page puts two things on the clipboard, and whatever you paste into takes the one it reads:

  • Markdown, as plain text, for an agent, a terminal or GitHub. Bullets, numbered steps, headings, tables, code fences, links and footnotes come out as Markdown, as if you'd copied that part of the source.
  • The page as drawn, for a mail or a document, which keep the formatting they're given.

You don't choose. Select and copy as you always do.

Here is what that selection pastes as, exactly as snyvi put it on the clipboard:

## Step 2: fetch and summarise

- Read `links.md` and keep the links added since the last digest.
- Fetch each page once, and skip any that fails (log it, don't retry).
- Write two lines per link: what it argues, and why it's on the list.

```bash
node digest.mjs --since last --out digests/2026-10-28.md
```

| Link kind | Fetched as | Kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Article | its readable text | title, two lines |
| Video | its description | title, length |
| Paper | its abstract | title, authors |

A whole section at once

Hover a heading and a small copy mark appears beside its #. It copies the heading and everything under it, down to the next heading at its level. That includes the footnotes it points to, which live at the end of the page. It's the quickest way to hand Claude one part of a long plan.

Download says where it went

Pictures, films, songs and PDFs got a Download in 1.51. Pressing it saved the file, and that was all: a check mark, and a word beside the middle of the picture, which for a tall one was the far corner of the window. You had to go and find the file.

Now the answer is where you pressed. The ↓ turns to a ✓, and beside it snyvi says where the file went, with Open and Show in folder right there.

There was a second reason it said nothing. The window that reports a download shipped in 1.52, but updating snyvi from inside the app kept the old window running. So for anyone who updated, Download drew its check mark and stayed quiet. An update now restarts a window older than 1.52. A release that changes the window without raising that minimum now fails CI, so this doesn't happen again.

What I left out

This covers Markdown pages, which is most of what agents write. A page that arrives as HTML copies the way any browser does, because there's no source in the right shape to give back.
